# Code of Conduct

This project welcomes respectful participation from collectors and developers of different backgrounds and experience levels.

- Treat people with respect; focus criticism on the work and explain it constructively.
- Do not harass, threaten, discriminate against or disclose private information about others.
- Do not post account credentials, private collection exports or photos without permission.
- Give useful context, welcome corrections, and respect the scope agreed for an issue or pull request.

These expectations apply to project issues, pull requests, discussions and other project-managed spaces.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, close disruptive threads, or restrict participation. Repeated or serious violations can result in exclusion from project spaces.

For an incident that cannot be handled safely in public, use GitHub's reporting tools. Avoid reposting sensitive or harmful material in a public issue. There is no guaranteed response time or dedicated moderation service at this prototype stage.
